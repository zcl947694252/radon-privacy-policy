一、引言

氡气检测（以下简称“本应用”）是一款通过蓝牙连接 ZD100 系列氡气检测工装、用于读取与展示氡气浓度、温湿度及设备状态的本地工具类应用。

本应用不包含网络连接功能（未申请互联网权限），不设用户账号，不上传任何数据到服务器，也不集成广告、统计或用户行为分析 SDK。您的检测数据仅在设备本地处理。

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日　|　适用版本：1.0.0　|　开发者：深圳汉威物联有限公司

二、我们如何处理信息

由于本应用完全在本地运行，我们不会收集、存储或传输任何可识别您个人身份的信息（如姓名、手机号、邮箱、身份证号、通讯录、短信、照片等）。

应用读取的数据（仅本地使用）

数据类别用途是否上传

三、权限说明

本应用仅申请以下系统权限，且均服务于蓝牙检测功能：

权限使用目的
蓝牙扫描 / 连接 / 广播（Android 12+ 为 BLUETOOTH_SCAN / CONNECT / ADVERTISE；Android 12 以下为 BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_ADMIN）发现并连接 ZD100 检测工装，下发校时、读取数据、修改设备名与蓝牙开关
定位权限（ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION；iOS 为 NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription）在部分系统中，扫描附近蓝牙低功耗（BLE）设备需要定位权限。本应用仅将其用于满足蓝牙扫描的系统要求，不会读取、记录或上传您的地理位置

您可以随时在系统设置中撤销上述权限，但撤销后本应用将无法连接检测工装。

四、数据导出与分享

本应用提供“导出历史数据为 CSV”功能：当您主动点击下载图标时，应用会向工装读取历史记录，生成包含时间与数值的 CSV 文件，写入应用的临时目录，并通过系统分享面板交由您选择保存位置或分享对象。

导出完全是用户主动触发的行为。文件去向由您决定，本应用不会自动上传、发送或保留该文件的副本。应用卸载或系统清理临时目录后，该文件将被删除。

五、第三方 SDK

本应用使用的第三方组件均为开源 Flutter 库，用于实现蓝牙通信、图表、权限申请、文件与分享等基础功能，不含广告、统计、崩溃上报或用户画像类 SDK：

  • flutter_blue_plus — 蓝牙低功耗通信

上述库仅在设备本地运行，不向第三方发送您的数据。

1. Introduction

Radon Guard (the "App") is a local utility application that connects via Bluetooth to ZD100-series radon detection devices to read and display radon concentration, temperature, humidity, and device status.

The App has no network capability (no Internet permission is requested), requires no user account, uploads no data to any server, and integrates no advertising, analytics, or user-behavior tracking SDKs. All detection data is processed locally on your device.

Effective date: November 19, 2026　|　Applicable version: 1.0.0　|　Developer: Shenzhen Hanwei IoT Co., Ltd. (深圳汉威物联有限公司)

2. How We Handle Information

Because the App operates entirely offline, we do not collect, store, or transmit any information that could identify you personally (such as name, phone number, email, ID number, contacts, messages, or photos).

3. Permissions

The App requests only the following permissions, all in support of the Bluetooth detection function:

PermissionPurpose
Bluetooth scan / connect / advertise (BLUETOOTH_SCAN / CONNECT / ADVERTISE on Android 12+; BLUETOOTH / BLUETOOTH_ADMIN on earlier versions)Discover and connect to ZD100 devices, perform time sync, read data, rename the device, and toggle Bluetooth

You may revoke these permissions at any time in system settings, though the App will then be unable to connect to the detection device.

4. Data Export & Sharing

The App offers a "export history to CSV" function: when you tap the download icon, the App reads history records from the device, generates a CSV file containing timestamps and values, writes it to the App's temporary directory, and hands it to the system share sheet for you to choose where to save or with whom to share.

Exporting is always user-initiated. You decide where the file goes; the App never automatically uploads, sends, or retains a copy. The file is removed when the App is uninstalled or the temporary directory is cleared by the system.

5. Third-Party SDKs

All third-party components are open-source Flutter libraries providing Bluetooth, charting, permissions, file, and sharing functions. No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or profiling SDKs are included:

  • flutter_blue_plus — Bluetooth Low Energy communication
  • provider — application state management
  • fl_chart — history charts
  • permission_handler — system permission requests
  • path_provider — access to the temporary directory
  • share_plus — system share sheet
  • flutter_localizations — multilingual localization

These libraries run locally and do not send your data to any third party.

6. Data Security & Children's Privacy

We collect no personal information, so there is no risk of personal data leakage. Detection data is processed only on your device. Please safeguard any CSV files you choose to export.

The App is an industrial detection tool, not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
